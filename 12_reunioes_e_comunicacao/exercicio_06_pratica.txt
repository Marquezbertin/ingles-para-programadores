============================================================
EXERCÍCIO 06 — Prática Combinando Tudo (Reuniões)
Dificuldade: Difícil
============================================================

OBJETIVO: Combinar habilidades de comunicação em reuniões.

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 1 — CENÁRIO: STANDUP COMPLETO
------------------------------------------------------------

Escreva seu standup completo (3 frases):

"Good morning everyone.
Yesterday ________________________________________
Today I will ________________________________________
I have/have no ________________________________________"

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 2 — CENÁRIO: SPRINT PLANNING
------------------------------------------------------------

Complete o diálogo de planning:

PM: "Let's look at the __________. We have 10 points
available. What should we pick up?"

Dev: "I recommend story #45 (search feature). It's
a __________ priority and worth 5 __________."

PM: "Any other suggestions?"

Dev 2: "We could also do story #48 (bug fix). That's
__________ points."

PM: "OK, let's take both into the __________. Any
__________?"

Respostas: backlog, high, points, 3/2, sprint, blockers

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 3 — CENÁRIO: RETRO
------------------------------------------------------------

Escreva uma contribuição para a retrospectiva:

"Personally, I think the sprint went __________ because
we __________. However, we could improve by __________.
One action item I'll take is __________."

Resposta (exemplo):
"Personally, I think the sprint went well because we
delivered all stories on time. However, we could improve
by estimating more accurately. One action item I'll
take is to track my time better."

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 4 — CENÁRIO: MEETING MINUTES
