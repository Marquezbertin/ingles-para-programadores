============================================================
EXERCÍCIO 01 — Daily Standup em Inglês
Dificuldade: Médio
============================================================

OBJETIVO: Aprender a participar de standups em inglês.

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 1 — ESTRUTURA DO STANDUP
------------------------------------------------------------

Cada pessoa fala 3 coisas:

1. O que fez ontem (past)
2. O que fará hoje (present/future)
3. Bloqueadores (if any)

Frases úteis:

PAST:
- "Yesterday I worked on..."
- "I finished..."
- "I fixed..."
- "I was debugging..."

PRESENT/FUTURE:
- "Today I'm working on..."
- "I'll be starting..."
- "I plan to..."

BLOCKERS:
- "I have a blocker..."
- "I'm stuck on..."
- "I need help with..."

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 2 — COMPLETE O STANDUP
------------------------------------------------------------

Dev:
"Good morning, everyone.

Yesterday I __________ on the search feature.
I __________ most of the code and __________ the
basic functionality. I also __________ a bug in the
database query.

Today I will __________ the search results page and
__________ the unit tests.

I have a __________: I need access to the staging
database to finish testing."

Respostas: worked, finished, implemented, fixed,
work on/build, write, blocker

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 3 — ESCREVA SEU STANDUP
------------------------------------------------------------

Escreva seu standup para hoje:

1. O que fez ontem:
"Yesterday I ________________________________________"

2. O que fará hoje:
"Today I will ________________________________________"

3. Algum bloqueador:
"________________________________________"

Resposta (exemplo):
"Yesterday I worked on the API integration.
Today I will write tests for the new endpoints.
I have no blockers today."

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 4 — VARIAÇÕES DE FRASES
------------------------------------------------------------

Reescreva mantendo o significado:

1. "Yesterday I worked on the login feature."
→ "Yesterday I was ________________________________________"

2. "Today I will fix the bug."
→ "Today I'm going to ________________________________________"

3. "I have a blocker with the database."
→ "I'm stuck because ________________________________________"

Respostas:
1. "Yesterday I was working on the login feature."
2. "Today I'm going to fix the bug."
3. "I'm stuck because I don't have database access."
