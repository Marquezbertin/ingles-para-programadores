============================================================
EXERCÍCIO 02 — Retrospectiva em Inglês
Dificuldade: Médio
============================================================

OBJETIVO: Participar de retrospectivas de sprint em inglês.

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 1 — ESTRUTURA DA RETRO
------------------------------------------------------------

| Seção | Pergunta |
|-------|----------|
| What went well | O que foi bem |
| What didn't go well | O que não foi bem |
| What we should try | O que devemos tentar |
| Action items | Itens de ação |

Frases úteis:

POSITIVO:
- "We delivered everything on time."
- "The team communicated well."
- "Testing improved a lot."

NEGATIVO:
- "We underestimated the story points."
- "We had too many interruptions."
- "The requirements were unclear."

MELHORIA:
- "We should try estimating more carefully."
- "Let's add more automated tests."
- "We need better documentation."

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 2 — COMPLETE A RETRO
------------------------------------------------------------

Facilitator: "Welcome to the sprint retrospective.
Let's start with what went well."

Dev 1: "We __________ all the stories in the sprint,
which is great. The team __________ well together."

Dev 2: "The new testing __________ helped us catch
bugs earlier."

Facilitator: "What didn't go well?"

Dev 3: "We had a few __________ with the deployment
pipeline. Also, the __________ for the client change
was unclear."

Facilitator: "What should we try?"

Dev 1: "I think we should __________ separate
deployment for staging and production."

Dev 2: "And let's __________ clarify requirements
before starting."

Respostas: completed, worked, framework, issues,
requirement, have/set up, make sure/always
