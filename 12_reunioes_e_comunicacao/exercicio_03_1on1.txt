============================================================
EXERCÍCIO 03 — One-on-one (1:1) em Inglês
Dificuldade: Médio
============================================================

OBJETIVO: Conduzir conversas 1:1 com seu gestor em inglês.

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 1 — TÓPICOS DE UMA 1:1
------------------------------------------------------------

| Tópico | Pergunta |
|--------|----------|
| Progresso | "How is your current project going?" |
| Obstáculos | "Are there any blockers?" |
| Desenvolvimento | "What skills do you want to develop?" |
| Feedback | "Do you have any feedback for me?" |
| Bem-estar | "How are you feeling about the workload?" |
| Carreira | "What are your career goals?" |

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 2 — FRASES PARA 1:1
------------------------------------------------------------

Use estas frases:

DISCUTINDO PROGRESSO:
- "I'm making good progress on..."
- "I'm behind schedule because..."
- "I'm waiting on..."

PEDINDO FEEDBACK:
- "Can you give me feedback on..."
- "How do you think I could improve..."
- "What should I focus on next?"

DISCUTINDO PROBLEMAS:
- "I'm struggling with..."
- "I need more time for..."
- "I would like to discuss..."

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 3 — COMPLETE O DIÁLOGO
------------------------------------------------------------

Manager: "How's your current project going?"

Dev: "I'm making good __________ on the new feature.
But I'm a bit __________ schedule on the deployment."

Manager: "What's causing the delay?"

Dev: "I'm __________ with the database migration.
I need more __________ to research the best approach."

Manager: "What skills do you want to __________?"

Dev: "I'd like to __________ more about cloud
infrastructure and __________ my testing skills."

Manager: "How are you feeling about the __________?"

Dev: "It's manageable, but the recent __________ have
been a bit much. I'm managing OK."

Respostas: progress, behind, struggling, time,
develop, learn, improve, workload, deadlines
