============================================================
EXERCÍCIO 03 — Descrever Processos em Inglês
Dificuldade: Médio
============================================================

OBJETIVO: Aprender a descrever processos de desenvolvimento em inglês.

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 1 — PROCESSO DE DESENVOLVIMENTO
------------------------------------------------------------

Descreva cada etapa:

1. "First, we __________ the requirements."
   (gather/collect)

2. "Then, we __________ the solution."
   (design)

3. "Next, we __________ the code."
   (write/implement)

4. "After that, we __________ the code."
   (review)

5. "Then, we __________ the feature."
   (test)

6. "Finally, we __________ to production."
   (deploy)

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 2 — DESCREVA O PROCESSO
------------------------------------------------------------

Escreva um parágrafo descrevendo o processo completo:

"First, we __________ the requirements from the
stakeholder. Then, we __________ the architecture
and create the __________. Next, I __________ the
code and write __________. After that, my teammate
____________ the PR. Then we __________ and finally
____________ to production."

Respostas: gather, design, tickets/issues, implement,
tests, reviews, test, deploy

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 3 — PROCESSOS ESPECÍFICOS
------------------------------------------------------------

Descreva cada processo:

1. Processo de fix de bug:
   "First, I __________ the bug. Then I __________ it
   locally. After that, I write a __________ to prevent
   it. Then I __________ a PR and wait for __________."
   Respostas: reproduce, fix, test, create, review

2. Processo de feature nova:
   "First, I __________ the ticket. Then I __________
   the approach. Next, I __________ the code. After
   that, I write __________. Then I __________ a PR."
   Respostas: pick up, plan/design, implement, tests, open
