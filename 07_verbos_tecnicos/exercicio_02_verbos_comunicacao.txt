============================================================
EXERCÍCIO 02 — Verbos de Comunicação
Dificuldade: Médio
============================================================

OBJETIVO: Aprender verbos usados em comunicação profissional.

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 1 — VOCABULÁRIO
------------------------------------------------------------

| Inglês     | Português  | Exemplo |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| Discuss    | Discutir   | "Let's discuss the approach." |
| Report     | Reportar   | "Report the issue to the team." |
| Estimate   | Estimar    | "Estimate the story points." |
| Prioritize | Priorizar  | "Prioritize the bug fixes." |
| Assign     | Atribuir   | "Assign this task to someone." |
| Track      | Rastrear   | "Track the progress." |
| Escalate   | Escalar    | "Escalate to the manager." |
| Clarify    | Esclarecer | "Clarify the requirements." |
| Confirm    | Confirmar  | "Confirm the meeting time." |
| Suggest    | Sugerir    | "Suggest improvements." |

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 2 — USE O VERBO CORRETO
------------------------------------------------------------

1. "Can you __________ what the client wants?"
   (clarify)

2. "I'll __________ the bug in Jira."
   (track/report)

3. "Let's __________ this in the next meeting."
   (discuss)

4. "Who should I __________ this task to?"
   (assign)

5. "I __________ this will take 3 days."
   (estimate)

6. "We need to __________ the critical bugs first."
   (prioritize)

7. "Please __________ that the server is running."
   (confirm)

8. "I __________ we use a caching strategy."
   (suggest)

9. "I need to __________ this to the tech lead."
   (escalate)

10. "I'll __________ the status to the PM."
    (report)

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 3 — DIALOGUE PRACTICE
------------------------------------------------------------

Complete o diálogo:

PM: "Can you __________ when the feature will be ready?"

Dev: "I'd __________ 5 story points. Let me __________
      the tasks in Jira."

PM: "Great. Please __________ the critical bugs first."

Dev: "I'll __________ them to the junior dev. Also,
      I'd like to __________ we add more tests."

PM: "Good __________. Please __________ the approach
      in the next standup."

Respostas: estimate, estimate, track, assign, suggest, suggestion, discuss
