PARTE 2 — CERIMÔNIAS ÁGEIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Descreva cada cerimônia em inglês:

1. Standup:
   "A short daily meeting where each team member
   shares what they did, what they will do, and
   any __________."
   Resposta: blockers

2. Sprint Planning:
   "A meeting at the beginning of the sprint to
   __________ which stories from the __________
   will be worked on."
   Resposta: plan, backlog

3. Retrospective:
   "A meeting at the end of the sprint to discuss
   what went well, what didn't, and what we can
   __________."
   Resposta: improve

4. Review/Demo:
   "A meeting to __________ the work completed
   during the sprint to stakeholders."
   Resposta: demo/show

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 3 — USER STORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Escreva user stories no formato:

"As a [role], I want [feature] so that [benefit]."

1. Como usuário, eu quero fazer login para acessar minha conta.
   → ________________________________________

2. Como admin, eu quero ver relatórios para tomar decisões.
   → ________________________________________

3. Como usuário, eu quero esquecer minha senha para recuperar o acesso.
   → ________________________________________

Respostas:
1. "As a user, I want to log in so that I can access my account."
2. "As an admin, I want to view reports so that I can make decisions."
3. "As a user, I want to reset my password so that I can regain access."
