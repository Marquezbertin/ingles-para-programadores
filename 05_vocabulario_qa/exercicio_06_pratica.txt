OBJETIVO: Combinar vocabulário QA em cenários reais.

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 1 — CENÁRIO: SPRINT COMPLETA
------------------------------------------------------------

Complete o relatório da sprint:

"This sprint, our team completed 5 __________ (stories)
with a total of 34 __________ (points). We delivered
2 new __________ (features) and fixed 3 __________ (bugs).

The __________ (velocity) of the team was consistent
with the last sprint. However, we had one __________ (blocker)
that delayed the deployment by 2 days."

Respostas: stories, points, features, bugs, velocity, blocker

------------------------------------------------------------
PARTE 2 — CENÁRIO: BUG REPORT COMPLETO
------------------------------------------------------------

Escreva um bug report completo:

Title: ________________________________________

Steps to reproduce:
1. ________________________________________
2. ________________________________________
3. ________________________________________

Expected result: ________________________________________
Actual result: ________________________________________
Environment: Chrome 120, Windows 11
Severity: ________________________________________

Resposta (exemplo):
Title: "Shopping cart total updates incorrectly when adding items"

Steps:
1. Add an item to the cart
2. Change the quantity to 2
3. Add another item
4. Check the total

Expected: Total = (item1 * 2) + item2
Actual: Total = item1 + item2 (quantity not multiplied)

Severity: Major
